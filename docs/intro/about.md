## What is Physical AI?

Physical AI is about creating intelligent systems that can interact with and manipulate the physical world. Unlike purely digital AI (like chatbots), Physical AI systems must:

- **Perceive** the world through sensors (cameras, LIDAR, IMU)
- **Reason** about physics, geometry, and causality
- **Act** on the world through motors and actuators
- **Handle uncertainty** in sensing and actuation

## Why This Matters

- **Robotics**: From warehouse automation to humanoids in homes
- **Autonomous vehicles**: Self-driving cars must perceive and navigate safely
- **Manufacturing**: Robots that can adapt to changing tasks
- **Healthcare**: Assistive robots and surgical systems
- **Space**: Planetary rovers and orbital robots
